What the lab demonstrates

Not just fixing access — understanding why it failed.

01

Read the evidence

Start with sign-in logs and failure reasons before changing passwords, MFA, groups, or assignments.

02

Check identity state

Review account status, MFA registration, device compliance, groups, and assigned applications.

03

Validate business context

Compare the request with role, department, HR context, and expected access for the user.

04

Confirm with the owner

Do not grant access only because someone asks. Confirm the approved access boundary.

05

Restore the right access

Make the smallest correct change in Entra, aligned with least privilege and the owner’s approval.

06

Verify and document

Ask the user to test again, then document the root cause and prevention step before closing.

Scenario shown

FinanceApp access failure

Emma Andersson cannot access FinanceApp. The sign-in evidence points to authorization rather than password or MFA. The lab guides the operator through identity state, business context, owner approval, Entra assignment, user verification, and lifecycle root cause.

  • Symptom: FinanceApp access denied
  • Evidence: Authorization requirement not satisfied
  • Decision: Normal FinanceApp access approved by owner
  • Action: Restore exact Entra app assignment
  • Closure: User verified and root cause documented
